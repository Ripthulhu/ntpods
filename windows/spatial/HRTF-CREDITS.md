# Measured ear profiles

The optional H3 and H20 profiles are measurements of two human listeners from
the SADIE II Database by Cal Armstrong, Lewis Thresh and Gavin Kearney,
Audio Lab, University of York. Copyright 2018 University of York.
The preparation script downloads the original 48 kHz, 256-tap SOFA files
without modification. The university distributes this version under the
[Apache License 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).

Dataset: [SADIE II, version 1.3, University of York](https://zenodo.org/records/12092466).
Publication: [A Perceptual Evaluation of Individual and Non-Individual HRTFs](https://doi.org/10.3390/app8112029).
The third choice is the existing Steam Audio built-in HRTF.
